# Ad copy review

**Verified on 2026-11-26 against** [Meta's Advertising Standards lesson](https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/learn/lessons/advertising-standards-best-practices). The editing rules are project conventions.

## Inputs and workflow

1. Read the brand voice, offer terms, audience context, destination and approved claim evidence. Identify the next action the copy promises.
2. Replace generic benefits and repeated introductions with concrete use cases or supported outcomes. Prefer direct verbs. Preserve the product's terminology, the author's intent and any required disclosure. Keep distinct concepts distinguishable.
3. Match the hook, offer, price, CTA, and destination. Keep material restrictions near the claim. Flag unsupported figures, endorsements, personal-attribute assumptions and guarantees for revision. Use a supported factual benefit when stronger proof is missing.
4. Read the draft in its placement preview. Put meaning before likely truncation and keep essential text clear of overlays.
5. Return the revised copy, unresolved evidence needs, and any preview or policy issues. Route creative packaging and policy review to the relevant platform skill. Keep edits local until the user approves a platform change via [approval gates](../ad-approval-gates/SKILL.md).
