# Meta Ads optimization

**Verified on 2026-11-26 against** [Meta Performance 5](https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/ads/performance-marketing) and [Meta Ads Manager tips](https://business-auth.facebook.com/business/tools/ads-manager/tips). See [source and decision notes](references/sources.md).

## Inputs

Collect comparable mature windows, ad or ad-set Insights, account targets, change history, effective delivery and learning state, destination metrics, and CRM outcomes when relevant.

## Workflow

1. Align metric definitions, currency, windows, placements and event lag with [reporting](../meta-ads-reporting/SKILL.md). Check actual delivery, tracking and funnel changes before ranking assets. Use comparable peers or the entity's own history for context.
2. Locate the change. Inspect CPM and mix, then outbound CTR and clicks, landing page views and on-site outcomes, then downstream quality. Show sample and spend with each rate. Use a stable denominator and check for changes in composition before attributing the result to an individual ad.
3. Flag likely fatigue when frequency rises and response deteriorates for the same ad and comparable delivery, after checking auction cost, audience mix, edits and measurement. A frequency change alone warrants investigation. Propose a replacement concept and a way to compare it. Check learning and recent significant edits before proposing further changes.
4. For B2B leads, use cost per qualified lead and the CRM qualification rate alongside form CPA. Compare Instant Forms and website forms on qualified outcomes after the same maturation lag. Review form expectations and CRM handoff before recommending spend changes.
5. Return observed change, supporting and conflicting evidence, likely cause, confidence, and one primary next action with a review window. When evidence is too thin for a decision, specify what will resolve it and record the current decision as monitor.

## Approval and routing

Start with read-only evidence. Use [approval gates](../../ads/ad-approval-gates/SKILL.md) for platform changes.

Route spend or bidding decisions to [budget and bidding](../meta-ads-budget-bidding/SKILL.md), event anomalies to [tracking](../meta-ads-tracking-attribution/SKILL.md), and no-delivery issues to [troubleshooting](../meta-ads-troubleshooting/SKILL.md).
