# Sources and decisions

Verified on 2026-11-26 against [Meta Advertising Standards guidance](https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/learn/lessons/advertising-standards-best-practices) and [Meta ad review guidelines](https://secure.facebook.com/business/ads/review-policy-guidelines?locale=en_GB).

- Meta explains personal-attribute restrictions, Special Ad Categories and applicable review practices. [Meta Advertising Standards guidance](https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/learn/lessons/advertising-standards-best-practices)
- Meta reviews ad components and the associated ad experience. Use the documented correction or review route. [Meta ad review guidelines](https://secure.facebook.com/business/ads/review-policy-guidelines?locale=en_GB)
- Meta's Blueprint lesson gives examples involving personal attributes and category declarations. It explains how to correct and resubmit an ad or request a review of an apparent mistake. [Meta Blueprint advertising guidelines](https://secure.facebook.com/business/learn/video/advertising-guidelines)
- Use the current Special Ad Categories and country contract; confirm that campaign and targeting settings are consistent. [Meta Campaign reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/adcampaigngroup/)
- v26 adds the explicit Advantage+ Audience field in a defined restricted ad-set creation case. [Meta Marketing API v26.0 changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/development/graph-api/changelog/version26.0)
- Meta ended political, electoral and social issue ads in the EU starting October 2025. Recheck current regional eligibility for these campaigns. [Meta EU ad eligibility announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/07/ending-political-electoral-and-social-issue-advertising-in-the-eu/)
