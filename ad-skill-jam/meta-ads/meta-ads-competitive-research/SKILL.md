# Meta Ads competitive research

**Verified on 2026-11-26 against** [Meta Ad Library](https://gl-es.facebook.com/ads/library). See [source and decision notes](references/sources.md).

## Inputs

Collect competitor or Page names, the target market, category, date of observation, and the research question. Use public links and a redacted scope if exports are supplied.

## Workflow

1. Search the Ad Library by market, relevant Page or term, and appropriate category. Record the filters, search date, and matched advertiser. Call out possible aliases or incomplete coverage.
2. For each useful example, record the source link or library reference, as-of date, observable creative, offer, format, messaging and destination when accessible. Group visually similar variants before counting concepts.
3. Distinguish observation from inference. State whether any spend or impression transparency applies to this category and market. Leave performance, ROAS, targeting and spend unavailable where the record supplies no evidence. Treat a long-running or repeated ad as a test hypothesis.
4. Return examples, observed patterns and original test ideas tied to the advertiser's offer and goals. Use only public evidence and lightweight manual or authorized reads.

## Approval and routing

Start with read-only evidence. Use [approval gates](../../ads/ad-approval-gates/SKILL.md) for platform changes.

Send distinct creative hypotheses to [creative](../meta-ads-creative/SKILL.md) and proposed comparisons to [experimentation](../meta-ads-experimentation/SKILL.md).
