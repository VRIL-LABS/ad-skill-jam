# Meta Ads creative

**Verified on 2026-11-26 against** [Meta placement and creative guidance](https://secure.facebook.com/business/ads/automation/tailored-campaigns) and [Meta ad preview reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/generatepreviews/). See [source and decision notes](references/sources.md).

## Inputs

Collect the objective, offer and actual destination, audience context, brand rules, substantiated claims, asset and creator rights, placement list, and existing ads or performance evidence.

## Workflow

1. Brief each concept with the viewer's job or problem, the offer, a supported benefit, visual proof, and the next action. Use concepts with different messages or demonstrations when testing distinct hypotheses.
2. Select a format that shows the proof clearly. Start Feed assets at 4:5 and Stories/Reels at 9:16 where the chosen format and placement support those ratios. Check current specs and placement previews for cropping, overlays, captions, audio and readability.
3. Apply [ad copy review](../../ads/ad-antislop/SKILL.md). Compare the visible price, conditions and CTA with the landing page or instant form on mobile. Follow the route through redirects or form confirmation and flag broken destinations. Verify partnership or asset permissions before a platform draft.
4. Review publisher identity, CTA, previewed text and enhancements. Inspect what Meta can alter or generate for this setup. For API work, read the [ledger](../../_shared/api-ledger/meta-ads.md) for poll and destination changes. Report unavailable previews as unverified.
5. Return concept, copy variants, assets and ratios, placement preview outcomes, claim evidence, destination and unresolved risks. Keep creative and upload plans as drafts until approved.

## Approval and routing

Start with read-only evidence. Use [approval gates](../../ads/ad-approval-gates/SKILL.md) for platform changes.

Route policy-sensitive claims or destinations to [compliance](../meta-ads-compliance-policy/SKILL.md) and controlled variants to [experimentation](../meta-ads-experimentation/SKILL.md).
