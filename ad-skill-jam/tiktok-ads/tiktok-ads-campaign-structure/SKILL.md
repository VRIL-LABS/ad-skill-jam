# TikTok Ads manual and smart+ campaign planning

Verified on 2026-11-26 against [Smart+ web campaigns](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/best-practices-for-smart-plus-web-campaigns). See [source and decision notes](references/sources.md) and the [API ledger](../../_shared/api-ledger/tiktok-ads.md).

## Inputs

Collect objective, advertiser and region, event source, asset rights, approved budget range and manual or Smart+ eligibility.

## Workflow

1. Map the objective, optimization event, budget owner and eligible placements. Check which settings remain controllable under the chosen campaign mode.
2. Assemble allowed ad groups and creative, including identity and landing destination. Record automated options and exclusions in the proposal.
3. Check the live account's eligible setup and current campaign controls. Stage paused or draft with verification before activation review.

## Output

Return the draft structure, dependency checklist, budget ownership and exact intended controls.

## Approval and routing

Start with authorized read-only data or redacted exports. Use [approval gates](../../ads/ad-approval-gates/SKILL.md) for account changes. Stage new entities paused or in draft when supported. Continue with [Spark Ads and creative testing](../tiktok-ads-creative/SKILL.md) when that work is needed.
