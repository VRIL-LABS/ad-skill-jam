# Sources and decisions

Verified on 2026-11-26 against [Google Ads policies](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6008942) and [Destination requirements](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6368661).

- Apply current policy to the offer, targeting and relevant geography. [Google Ads policies](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6008942)
- Review destination functionality, accessibility, quality and the promoted content. [Destination requirements](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6368661)
- Use the genuine destination and approved account. Google prohibits attempts to circumvent review. [Circumventing systems policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15938075)
- Use Google's documented correction or appeal process. [Google Ads policy review and appeal](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7501932)
