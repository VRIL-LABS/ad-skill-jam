# Sources and decisions

Verified on 2026-11-26 against [Search delivery troubleshooting](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9208915) and [About Quality Score](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167118).

- Use delivery evidence and inspect changes before attributing causes. [Search delivery troubleshooting](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9208915)
- Quality Score is a keyword diagnostic with three components. Google does not use that reported score as an auction input. [About Quality Score](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167118)
- Check which impression-share cuts are available before separating budget and rank constraints. [Impression share reporting](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7103314)
- Base a Smart Bidding comparison on mature conversion cycles and account targets. [Evaluate automated bidding](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10167267)
